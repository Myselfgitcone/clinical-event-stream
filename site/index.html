Care gaps after a type 2 diabetes diagnosis

Two things guidelines expect soon after diagnosis: a statin order within a year for ages 40 to 75, and an HbA1c result within 90 days for everyone. Both are read from the gold star schema, by payer type and by age band.

What was left out, and why

Rows that failed their contract are kept with the raw payload and a reason, never dropped silently. Resource types the project does not model yet are counted so the gap is visible.

How the data gets here

Weekly, from a GitHub Actions runner. One command runs every stage; CI runs the same stages on a 40-patient cohort before anything merges.

SyntheaA seeded cohort of FHIR R4 bundles, byte-for-byte reproducible.
ContractsOne YAML per resource type. Passes go to bronze, failures to quarantine.
De-identificationAll 18 Safe Harbor classes, one test each. Silver is pseudonymous.
dbt on DuckDB27 models, 91 tests, a star schema and the two marts above.
Feeds and Azuresummary.json and weekly.json here; bronze, silver and gold in ADLS Gen2.
